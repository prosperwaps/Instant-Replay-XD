Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply after the deadline?

Late applications are considered only if places remain in the requested class. Contact the admissions office before submitting a late application.

Are scholarships or fee reductions available?

Yes. Merit and need-based support is available for a limited number of students each year. You can indicate interest during enrolment after receiving an offer.

Is boarding available for every class?

Boarding is available from Form 1 to Upper Sixth, subject to dormitory capacity. Boarding requests are confirmed in the order applications are received.

What if I don't have all the documents yet?

The transfer certificate is optional at this stage. For other missing documents, contact the admissions office with your reference number after submitting.

How is the entrance assessment used?

It checks grade-appropriate English and Mathematics and is used to confirm class placement. It is not a pass/fail examination.
